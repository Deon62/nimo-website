Privacy Policy

Last updated: 21 November 2026

Nimo is a voice app that lets you have spoken conversations with AI characters. It is operated by Ardena (“we”, “us”). This policy explains what we collect, why, who we share it with and the choices you have. We follow the Kenya Data Protection Act, 2019.

1. Information we collect

  • Account details: your name, email address, phone number and profile photo. If you sign in with Google or Apple, we receive your name and email from them.
  • Voice and conversations: when you are on a call, the audio of what you say is sent to our servers to be transcribed, and the text of the conversation is used to create replies. We keep the transcript of each call in your call history.
  • Call recordings: only if you turn on recording during a call (paid plans). Recordings are saved to your account.
  • Payment information: your plan, amount paid, payment reference and status. For M-Pesa we receive your phone number and M-Pesa receipt number. Card payments are handled by Paystack: we never see or store your full card number or security code.
  • Usage: which characters you call, when, and for how long, so we can apply your plan’s daily minutes and show your usage.
  • Camera: during a video call your camera preview is shown on your device only. It is not recorded or sent to us.

2. How we use it

  • To run your account and sign you in.
  • To hold conversations: transcribe your speech, generate replies and speak them in the character’s voice.
  • To process payments and give you the features of your plan.
  • To keep Nimo safe, fix problems and improve the service.
  • To contact you about your account or important changes.

We do not sell your personal information, and we do not use your conversations for advertising.

4. How long we keep it

  • Account details: until you delete your account.
  • Calls, transcripts and recordings: until you delete them or your account.
  • Payment records: for as long as tax and financial laws require, after which they are deleted.

5. Your rights

You can ask to access, correct or delete your personal data, object to or restrict how we use it, and ask for a copy of it. You can delete individual calls in the app and delete your account at any time. To exercise any right, email support@ardena.co.ke. You can also complain to the Office of the Data Protection Commissioner (odpc.go.ke).

6. Security

Data is encrypted in transit, access to it is restricted, and payment details are handled by licensed payment providers. No system is perfectly secure, so please keep your sign-in details private.

7. Children

Nimo is for people aged 18 and over. We do not knowingly collect information from children. If you believe a child has used Nimo, contact us and we will delete their data.

8. About AI conversations

Replies are generated by AI and can be wrong. Nimo’s characters are not doctors, lawyers, financial advisers or therapists, and Nimo is not an emergency service. If you are in danger, contact local emergency services.

9. Changes

We may update this policy. If the changes are significant we will tell you in the app or by email before they take effect.
