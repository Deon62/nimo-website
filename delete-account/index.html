Delete your Nimo account

Nimo by Ardena

How to request deletion

  1. Email support@ardena.co.ke from the email address on your account, with the subject “Delete my Nimo account”. If you signed up with a phone number, include that number.
  2. We’ll confirm the request. To protect you, we may ask you to verify that the account is yours.
  3. Your account and data are deleted within 30 days of confirmation.

Request account deletion

What is deleted

  • Your profile: name, email, phone number and photo.
  • Your call history, transcripts and call recordings.
  • Saved payment methods and your plan.

What we keep

  • Payment records (amount, date, reference and receipt) for as long as tax and financial laws require. These are not used for anything else.

Delete only some data

You can delete individual calls in the app at any time, without deleting your account. To ask us to delete other specific data, email support@ardena.co.ke.
