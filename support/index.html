Support

We usually reply within one working day.

Email support@ardena.co.ke with your question. Please include the email or phone number on your account, and your phone model if something isn’t working.

Common questions

How do daily minutes work?
Each plan includes a number of call minutes per day. Free has 10, Plus has 60, and Pro is unlimited.

I paid with M-Pesa but my plan didn’t change.
It can take a minute to confirm. If it still hasn’t updated, email us your M-Pesa receipt number.

Does my plan renew automatically?
No. Each payment covers one month or one year. Pay again in the app to continue.

Can I delete my calls?
Yes. Open a call in your call history and choose Delete. To delete everything, see Delete your account.

Is the AI always right?
No. Replies are generated by AI and can be wrong, so check anything important with a qualified person.
